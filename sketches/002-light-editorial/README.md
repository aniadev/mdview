# Variant: Light Editorial (B)

## Design stance

A quiet, print-like reading experience — because mdview's other half is *writing*. Serif headlines, paper-white background, ruled-line notebook mock. Speaks to writers and journalers first, developers second.

## Key choices

- **Layout:** centered single-column hero with a "paper" split-pane mock (editor left / rendered right, ruled lines), numbered editorial feature list, journal section with skeleton date-card
- **Typography:** Newsreader (serif) for display, Inter for UI, JetBrains Mono for code — editorial + engineering blend
- **Color:** warm paper palette — `#faf9f7` bg, ink `#1c1917`, deep green accent `#0a5c36` (a deliberate departure from the app blue — paper/ink vibe), amber `#c9a227` for numerals
- **Interaction:** scroll-reveal fades, hover on pills/links. Deliberately no heavy animation — the content is the motion
- **Animation:** none beyond gentle fades — "quiet" is the brand statement

## Trade-offs

- Strong at: differentiation, writer/journaler audience, premium editorial feel
- Weak at: "terminal/PTY/graph" features feel slightly out of place in this register; devs may read it as soft

## Best for

A brand that wants to own the *writing* side of markdown — journaling, knowledge bases, personal notes. Differentiates hard from the sea of dark dev-tool pages.
